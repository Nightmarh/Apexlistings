FAQs

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before you get started with Apex Listings.

How long does it take to build a website?
+

Typically 1–3 weeks for a Starter site, 2–5 weeks for Growth, and 4–10 weeks for Premium projects — depending on complexity and how quickly content and feedback is provided.

Do I need to provide my own content?
+

We can work with content you provide, or offer copywriting and photography sourcing as an add-on. We'll guide you through exactly what we need at the start of the project.

Will my website work on mobile phones?
+

Absolutely. Every site we build is fully mobile-responsive and rigorously tested across multiple devices and browsers before going live. Mobile is always our first priority.

Can I update the website myself after launch?
+

Yes! Where applicable, we use a CMS (like WordPress or Webflow) so you can easily update text, images, and blog posts without any coding knowledge whatsoever.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
+

Yes — every plan includes free support for 1–6 months. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance packages covering security updates, backups, and edits.

What payment methods do you accept?
+

We accept bank transfer, PayPal, and major debit/credit cards. Typically we split payment 50% upfront to begin work and 50% upon final delivery and launch.
